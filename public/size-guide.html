How to measure: Bust — fullest part of bust, keeping tape parallel to floor. Waist — narrowest part of natural waist. Hip — fullest part of hips, about 8" below waist.

These are standard US dress sizes. Each House of Tarab piece is made to order — if you fall between sizes or have specific fit requirements, we recommend booking a custom appointment for in-person measurements.
