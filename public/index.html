Our Philosophy

Tarab

◆

Tarab (طرب) is the emotional ecstasy evoked by art — the moment a piece of music, a line of poetry, or a garment moves you beyond words. It is what we chase in every stitch.

House of Tarab rejects the disposable rhythms of fast fashion. Each piece is made to order, crafted from finer materials with an attention to detail that cannot be rushed. We do not chase trends. We build garments that make the wearer feel something extraordinary — the way a room stills when the right woman walks in.

"For the rooms that remember you."
