Les Éternelles

Welcome to Les Éternelles — the beating heart of House of Tarab.

We begin with Le Cintré — "The Fitted One." A sculpted silhouette born from the architectural curve of the corset and the golden restraint of 1950s daywear. It is our first release, and it sets the tone for everything to come.

Les Éternelles are not seasonal whims. They are our permanent 10 — timeless silhouettes that remain unchanged in form, year after year. What shifts is the mood: the palette that dances with spring's butter yellows, summer's lavenders, and autumn's deeper reveries.

These are the dresses you return to. The ones that hold their shape as the world turns, adapting their color to your light. Eternal in cut. Seasonal in soul.

Signature — From $260

The Fitted One

Refined duality. This two-piece semi-couture set unites a sculpted corset top with a waist-defining pencil skirt—both engineered with a structured crepe shell over a pure silk lining that breathes against the skin.

The corset features ultralight platinum boning for luxurious, unbending support, cinched perfectly via the fully adjustable back lace. The square neckline and short sleeves keep the silhouette powerful and modern.

Inside, every silk seam is hand-stitched to move organically with your body, while the crepe exterior is machine-finished for razor-sharp, architectural lines. Custom-drafted to your exact waist, this is the pinnacle of power daywear for the modern businesswoman—uncompromising structure, effortless comfort, and refined femininity that commands every room.

6–10 week wait time. You'll receive progress photos every 2 weeks as your piece is made.
